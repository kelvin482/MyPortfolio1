Small teams can apply zero trust without enterprise complexity. The key is to start with identity, harden endpoints, and segment access around business risk.

## What Changed in 2026

- MFA coverage is now expected across all privileged and developer workflows.
- Device trust checks are becoming standard before granting app access.
- Teams are moving from network-based trust to identity- and policy-based trust.

## Practical Baseline for Small Teams

- Enforce phishing-resistant MFA for admin and engineering accounts.
- Use role-based access with least privilege as default.
- Separate production access from daily collaboration tools.
- Add endpoint controls for patching, disk encryption, and malware protection.

## 7-Day Rollout Plan

- Day 1-2: Inventory users, apps, and privileged access paths.
- Day 3-4: Enforce MFA and remove shared credentials.
- Day 5: Configure role-based access groups.
- Day 6: Lock production access behind policy checks.
- Day 7: Review logs and tune alerts for high-risk activity.

## Final Takeaway

Zero trust is not a product; it is an access model. For small teams, consistent policy and clean identity management give the highest return with the lowest overhead.
